# WebLivePhoto

The Live Photo preview interaction and layered image/video presentation in
`src/components/LivePhotoViewer.vue` are adapted from
https://github.com/link9596/WebLivePhoto (livephoto.js blob
`9de238ca54d8dae3ebf52d8a4d34f204a3806b57`, livephoto.css blob `a2300c225dcc79ba72bba6eda27df10b34372bb3`).
The Vue lifecycle, signed-resource loading, retry, accessibility and gesture
cancellation are integrated with Yearly. No external runtime script is loaded.
